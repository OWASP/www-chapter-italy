# OWASP Precogly: Open-Source Threat Modeling for the AI-Coding Era

## Vikramaditya Narayan

As software development becomes increasingly AI-assisted, threat models must remain structured, reproducible, auditable, and understandable by humans.

This session introduces [OWASP Precogly](https://github.com/precogly/precogly), an open-source platform designed to support repeatable and AI-agent-ready threat modeling.

The presentation will explore how Precogly combines visual data-flow modelling with structured libraries that connect system components, threats, and countermeasures to established sources such as STRIDE, CWE, CAPEC, and MITRE ATT&CK.

Participants will see how Precogly can support:

* visual creation of data-flow diagrams;
* systematic identification and management of threats;
* reusable and community-curated threat-model libraries;
* traceability between threats, countermeasures, and compliance requirements;
* TM-BOM import and export;
* integration with engineering workflows and AI agents;
* human review of AI-assisted threat-model outputs.

The session will show how an open-source approach can help organizations move from occasional threat-modeling exercises to a continuous and maintainable engineering practice.

### Speaker bio

Vikramaditya Narayan is the creator and Project Leader of OWASP Precogly, an open-source platform for repeatable and AI-agent-ready threat modeling.

He holds a Master’s degree from Carnegie Mellon University and is a Certified Threat Modeling Professional and Azure-certified AI Engineer. His work focuses on the intersection of threat modeling, AI-assisted software development, AI agents, and security engineering.

# The Cyber Resilience Act: From Regulatory Requirements to Security Practice

## Elena Musetti

The European Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) is transforming cybersecurity from a best practice into a product requirement. Organizations developing products with digital elements must adopt secure-by-design principles, manage vulnerabilities throughout the product lifecycle, and improve transparency across their software supply chains. While the regulation defines the expected outcomes, many organizations are still determining how to translate these requirements into practical development and security activities. This session will introduce the CRA, focusing on the key obligations
for manufacturers and the impact on software development processes. Building on these foundations, the session will present a practical roadmap showing how established OWASP projects can help bridge the gap between compliance and
implementation.

### Speaker bio

Elena Francesca Musetti is a Quality Assurance Engineer in the medical device industry.
With a background in Physics, Computer Science, and Tech Law, she specializes in
regulatory compliance and software development lifecycle management for Software as
a Medical Device (SaMD). 

Her professional and research interests focus on the impact
of emerging regulations, particularly the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), on the medical
device sector.
